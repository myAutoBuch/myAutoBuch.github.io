Privacy policy

Please note: This is a courtesy translation of the German privacy policy. myAutoBuch is operated from Germany and subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and German law. In case of any discrepancy, the German version is legally binding.

We take the protection of your data seriously. This privacy policy tells you which data is processed when you use myAutoBuch, how that data is stored and what rights you have.

2. General information on data processing

In its current version, myAutoBuch can generally be used offline. Vehicle, maintenance, consumption, photo and document data is stored exclusively locally on your phone by default.

Your vehicle data stored in the app is currently not transmitted automatically to us or to any third-party servers. We have no access to your locally stored vehicle data, registration numbers, VINs, photos, documents, costs, insurance details or service histories.

Internet access may only be required for optional functions, for example:

  • voluntarily sharing usage statistics via Microsoft Forms,
  • opening the privacy policy or the legal notice,
  • external links,
  • contacting us by email,
  • joining the iOS waiting list via the website.

Planned extension: A future version may offer optional cloud synchronisation as a premium feature. This function is expressly voluntary and will never be activated without your active consent. As soon as it becomes available, this privacy policy will be extended with the specific processing details.

3. Local data storage in the app

In the current version, all data you enter in myAutoBuch is stored locally on your device. This may include in particular:

  • vehicle master data,
  • registration numbers, if you enter them,
  • vehicle identification numbers/VIN, if you enter them,
  • maintenance and repairs,
  • fuel entries and consumption data,
  • photos, receipts and documents,
  • inspection data,
  • insurance data,
  • accident and damage data,
  • cost information,
  • financing or loan information, if you enter it,
  • reminders and maintenance plans.

This data is not transmitted to us automatically. The security of this locally stored data also depends on your device's security settings, for example screen lock, operating system protection and backup settings.

You can delete, export or back up data yourself within the app. Exported backups and PDF files may contain personal vehicle data. Please store such files securely and only share them deliberately with people or organisations you trust.

4. Legal basis

Where you use myAutoBuch exclusively locally on your device and do not actively transmit any data to us, we as the provider do not process this locally stored app data.

For optional functions where data is actively transmitted to us or to a service provider, the legal basis stated in each case applies. This concerns in particular:

  • voluntary sharing of usage statistics: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR,
  • contact by email: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR,
  • joining the iOS waiting list: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR,
  • future optional cloud synchronisation: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

You can withdraw any consent given at any time with effect for the future.

5. App permissions

Depending on your device, Android version and the function used, myAutoBuch may request the following permissions:

  • Camera: the camera can be used to take photos of receipts, documents or vehicles directly.
  • Photos, media, files or file selection: this permission or the system's own picker can be used to select, store or link photos, receipts, PDF files, backups or export files to a vehicle entry. Wherever possible, myAutoBuch uses the system's own pickers so that no general access to all files is required.
  • Notifications: notifications can be used to remind you about inspection dates, insurance, maintenance or other vehicle-related dates. Notifications are only used if you allow them.
  • Internet access: internet access may be required for optional functions, in particular for voluntarily sharing usage statistics via Microsoft Forms, opening the privacy policy or legal notice, external links and contacting us.

6. Voluntary usage statistics

myAutoBuch contains a function that lets you voluntarily submit usage statistics. This function is disabled by default and is only triggered if you actively use the “Share feedback” button.

The transmission takes place via Microsoft Forms into an evaluation table managed by us. The provider of Microsoft Forms is Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd.

What is transmitted:

  • the number of your vehicles,
  • the number of your service entries,
  • the number of your maintenance plans,
  • the number of your fuel-ups,
  • the number of your photos,
  • the number of your inspection/accident entries,
  • the number of your insurance policies,
  • the number of your loans,
  • optional information about how long you have used the app,
  • optional information about how often you use it.

What is not transmitted:

  • no names,
  • no email addresses,
  • no registration numbers,
  • no VINs,
  • no vehicle identifiers,
  • no photos,
  • no documents,
  • no specific costs,
  • no invoices or receipts,
  • no maintenance details,
  • no location data.

The transmitted data contains no directly identifying information such as name, email address, registration number, VIN, photos, documents or specific costs. It is used exclusively for the statistical evaluation of app usage and for improving myAutoBuch. We do not intend to attribute it to any specific person.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, i.e. your voluntary consent by actively triggering the function.

Please note: when using Microsoft Forms, technical data may be processed by Microsoft. You can find more information in Microsoft's privacy information.

7. No tracking and no active advertising

In the current version, myAutoBuch contains no tracking tools, no analytics frameworks such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics, no active advertising and does not use advertising IDs.

There is no automatic analysis of your usage behaviour.

Should advertising, analytics functions, advertising IDs or comparable services be introduced in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly in advance.

8. External links

myAutoBuch may contain links to external websites, for example to the privacy policy, the legal notice, Microsoft Forms or contact options.

When you open an external link, you leave the app or open an external website or service. The respective provider is responsible for the processing of personal data on external websites or by external services.

9. Contact by email

If you contact us by email at myAutoBuch@outlook.de, we process the information you provide exclusively to handle your enquiry.

This may include in particular:

  • your email address,
  • your name, if you provide it,
  • the content of your message,
  • technical information that you provide voluntarily.

Your data is not passed on to third parties unless this is necessary to handle your enquiry or required by law.

Your enquiry is deleted as soon as it is no longer required for processing and no statutory retention obligations prevent this.

Depending on the content of the enquiry, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

10. Google Play Store

When you download the app via the Google Play Store, data is processed by Google, for example your user account, device information, IP address and download time. We have no influence on this processing.

You can find more information in Google's privacy policy:
Google privacy policy

11. iOS waiting list

We use Microsoft Forms for signing up to the iOS waiting list on our website. The provider is Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd.

Only the data you voluntarily enter in the form is processed. This may include in particular:

  • email address,
  • optionally your first name,
  • optional voluntary information,
  • the time of submission.

This data is used exclusively for:

  • notifying you as soon as an iOS version is available,
  • organising the waiting list,
  • improving myAutoBuch,
  • voluntary follow-up questions about the app, if required.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by contacting us at myAutoBuch@outlook.de.

12. Future cloud synchronisation

A future version of myAutoBuch may offer optional cloud synchronisation. This function is currently not part of standard local use.

Should cloud synchronisation be introduced, the following applies:

  • it will only be activated after the user's active consent,
  • it is voluntary,
  • it may require additional data processing,
  • this privacy policy will be extended accordingly before it is introduced,
  • the specific providers, data types, storage locations, security measures and legal bases will then be stated transparently.

No cloud synchronisation will be activated without your active consent.

13. Backups and export

myAutoBuch may contain functions for backing up, exporting or restoring data. Such functions are triggered actively by you.

Exported files, backups or PDF reports may contain personal vehicle data, for example registration number, VIN, service history, costs, photos or document information.

Please store such files securely and only share them deliberately. If you store, send or place exported files outside the app or in cloud services, further processing is governed by the respective storage location or service.

14. Deleting data

You can delete all data stored locally in the app yourself at any time by:

  • removing individual entries directly in the app,
  • deleting vehicles within the app,
  • uninstalling the app,
  • clearing the app storage in the Android system settings.

Please note: when you delete the app or its storage, locally stored data may be permanently lost if you have not created a backup beforehand.

Usage statistics that have already been submitted voluntarily contain no directly identifying information. Individual attribution and deletion of single statistical records may therefore not be possible.

If you have submitted personal data via the iOS waiting list, the contact form or by email, you can contact us at myAutoBuch@outlook.de to request deletion.

15. Storage period

Data stored locally in the app remains on your device until you delete it yourself, uninstall the app or clear the app storage.

Enquiries submitted by email are stored only for as long as necessary to process them or as required by statutory retention obligations.

Data from the iOS waiting list is stored only for as long as it is required to organise the waiting list, notify you about an iOS version or improve the app, unless you withdraw your consent beforehand.

Voluntarily submitted usage statistics are used for statistical evaluation to improve the app.

16. Your rights

Within the scope of the statutory requirements you have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • right of access under Art. 15 GDPR,
  • right to rectification under Art. 16 GDPR,
  • right to erasure under Art. 17 GDPR,
  • right to restriction of processing under Art. 18 GDPR,
  • right to data portability under Art. 20 GDPR,
  • right to object under Art. 21 GDPR,
  • right to withdraw consent under Art. 7(3) GDPR,
  • right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority under Art. 77 GDPR.

To exercise your rights, you can contact us at the following address:

myAutoBuch@outlook.de

Please note: we have no access to data stored exclusively locally on your device. You can manage and delete this data directly in the app or via your device.

17. Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

If you believe that the processing of your personal data violates data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

You may in particular contact the supervisory authority responsible for your place of residence.
