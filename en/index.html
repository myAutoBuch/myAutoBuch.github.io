Frequently asked questions

Is myAutoBuch really free?+

The download stays free forever. Optional premium features will follow later – the core of the app remains free.

Where is my data stored?+

All data stays locally on your phone. There is no server and no account. An optional cloud backup will follow later as a premium feature.

Is the app available in English?+

Yes – myAutoBuch is fully available in English. By default the app follows your device language, and you can switch between English and German at any time under Profile → Language.

Which country is the app made for?+

myAutoBuch was originally built for Germany and still contains a few German-specific fields (HSN/TSN codes, TÜV/HU inspection dates and no-claims classes). Everything else – maintenance, fuel, costs, receipts and reminders – works anywhere, and units, currency and date format are freely configurable.

Can I use miles, gallons and my own currency?+

Yes. You can switch between kilometres and miles, litres and gallons (US/UK), L/100km and mpg, and choose your currency and date format under Profile.

Which vehicles are supported?+

Currently cars only. Motorcycles, motorhomes and trucks will follow in later versions.

Is there an iPhone version?+

myAutoBuch launches on Android first. An iOS version is not ruled out – join the iOS waiting list and we will let you know as soon as there is news.

Where do I get the app?+

myAutoBuch is available for free on the Google Play Store – just search for “myAutoBuch” or use the download button above.
