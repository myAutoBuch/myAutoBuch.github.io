What's new

All myAutoBuch updates – new features, improvements and bug fixes. The latest version is at the top.

Version 1.5

October 2026

New
  • Workshop mode per vehicle: tyres, spare parts, torques, fluids and your garage in one place.
  • Motorcycles are supported – with their own lists and 2 wheels in the tyre manager.
  • Tyres: measure tread, wear forecast and purchase price as a separate cost category.
  • Spare parts with part numbers, price and source – tap the source to open it.
  • Statistics: vehicle value forecast and cost per 100 km, optionally including depreciation.
  • Automatic backup on your phone – the last 3 versions are kept.
  • Accidents: record the amount you paid yourself.
Improved
  • Fuel: clearer form with required fields and a warning for implausible odometer readings.
  • CSV export for all areas; your own entries appear in the selection lists; “Rate app” in the menu.
Fixed
  • Safer import, update and restore, more accurate calculations (consumption, mileage, miles, loan) and many small fixes.

Version 1.4

September 2026

New
  • New “Costs” tab per vehicle: vehicle tax, car care, parking, tolls or a fine – every entry counts automatically in statistics, the yearly review and the PDF.
  • The app is now available in English – you can switch the language under Profile (default: your device language).
  • Second fuel for bi-fuel vehicles (e.g. petrol + LPG/autogas).
  • Updates straight from the app: as soon as a newer version is available, you can install it with one tap.
  • Extended tyre management: summer, winter and all-weather tyres can be recorded separately – if you only use all-weather tyres, simply mark the others as “not needed”.
  • “Invite friends”: share the app with one tap – and unlock new achievements for it.
Improved
  • Clearer cost statistics: costs by category with expandable details and the current year preselected.
  • The mileage is now required for fill-ups, service, inspection and accidents – for more reliable reports.
  • More reliable saving, backups and imports – your data is better protected even when storage is full, and export/import now report honestly whether everything was really saved.
  • Smoother start and many refinements around dates, units and consumption; new maintenance templates “Summer check” and “Winter check”.
Fixed
  • Camera: better photo quality and much more reliable – the photo and the data you already entered are kept even if Android briefly restarts the app while taking the photo.
  • Attached PDF receipts can now be opened (via your phone’s PDF app).
  • More accurate fuel consumption – also when you refuel twice on the same day; no more unnecessary prompt in that case.
  • Status bar (clock/battery): the tab bar now stays neatly below it while scrolling.

Version 1.3

August 2026

New
  • New “Profile” menu entry: you can now set how many days in advance reminders (inspection, insurance, maintenance) reach you.
  • New “Spare parts” tab per vehicle: record which parts fit (oil filter, brake pads, battery …) – plus oil type and oil quantity.
  • Settings (Profile): currency (€/$/£) and date format freely selectable.
  • Settings (Profile): distance in kilometres or miles – display and input convert automatically.
  • Settings (Profile): fuel quantity in litres or gallons (US/UK) and consumption in L/100km or mpg – liquid fuels convert automatically, electricity and gas stay unchanged.
  • Annual mileage: enter your agreed mileage limit with your insurance – a progress bar (insurance tab and statistics) shows where you stand and warns you early if you are on track to exceed it.
  • New “Trips” tab: a simple trip log per vehicle – record trips with start/end odometer and purpose (business/private/commute); distance and shares are calculated automatically. Note: this is not a tax-compliant logbook.
  • Legal notice: a short disclaimer appears when you first open the app (readable any time under “Profile → Legal”); the PDF overview carries a matching footer note.
Improved
  • Vehicle health: the “Documentation & receipts” building block is now called “History & profile” – a better match for what it measures.
Fixed
  • The status bar (clock/battery) is no longer overlapped by content on current Android versions – there is now a solid bar at the top.

Version 1.2

August 2026

Fixed
  • Display on newer Android versions corrected – content no longer overlapped the status bar (clock/battery).
  • “Fuel cost/100 km” is now calculated correctly (previously too low) and has a clearer name.
  • Multiple fuel-ups on the same day are now sorted correctly by odometer reading.
  • Year in review: the year selection now only shows years of the selected vehicle.
  • The reminder counter in the menu now matches the colour-coded due dates.

Version 1.1

July 2026

New
  • New dashboard card “Important today” – all urgent due dates at a glance.
  • CSV export for fuel, service, inspection, accidents, insurance and finance – straight into Excel & co.
  • Checklists for maintenance plans: tick them off and save them as proof in the service entry.
  • Reminders now show separate progress bars for time and distance.
  • Service history as a clear timeline – with filter, sorting and yearly totals.
  • New price trend chart per litre in the statistics (last 30 fuel-ups).
  • Every fuel-up is marked as a full or partial fill – the basis for accurate consumption.
  • Custom vehicle make can be entered freely (as already possible for the model).
  • Unlocked achievements are now celebrated – with animation and confetti.
  • “What's new” – this overview shows you the changes in every version.
Improved
  • Vehicle health is clearer – with an explanation of how the score is calculated.
  • New achievement icons and a tidier achievements page with unlock dates.
  • Maintenance plans are more compact, with colour-coded due status; every service type has its own icon.
  • Completely reworked PDF export: cover page with vehicle photo, calmer layout, easy-to-read yearly overview.
  • The vehicle export now also contains the stored insurance documents.
Fixed
  • Photos were lost when a form was opened and then cancelled.
  • Images opened outside the app – they now appear in the gallery.
  • Due maintenance was not counted in the side menu when only the distance was close.
  • Amounts with a thousands separator (e.g. “15,000”) were read incorrectly – now correct.
  • An odometer reading such as “150,000” was sometimes stored as 150 – fixed.
  • Once unlocked, achievements no longer disappear when the score drops over time.
  • The year in review mixed up the distance driven when several vehicles were present.
  • CNG/natural gas is now recorded in kg instead of litres.
  • For electric cars the fuel station ranking showed “€/L” instead of kWh.
